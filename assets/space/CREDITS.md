# Cosmic Match observation imagery

All assets are stored locally for offline play. NASA DONKI supplies the event facts;
SDO and SOHO imagery supplies the observations shown on the cards. The footage is
archival, not a live solar feed. Colorized telescope channels are not natural color.

| Local asset | Original asset and source | Credit and preparation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `flare.jpg` | [X8_Still_1.jpg](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a010000/a012700/a012706/X8_Still_1.jpg), [NASA SVS 12706](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/12706) | NASA/GSFC/SDO. September 10, 2017 X8.2 flare, blended 171/304 angstrom channels. Resized to 1280×720. |
| `solar-loop.mp4` | [Original 960×540 WebM](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a010000/a012700/a012706/12706_SDO_X8_Flare_171-304_Compatible.webm), same SVS page | NASA/GSFC/SDO. Original seconds 4–18; slowed to 1.5 times the original duration; resized to 854 pixels wide; silent H.264, 20 fps. |
| `cloud.jpg` | [sept_soho.gif](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/sept_soho.gif), [NASA observation explanation](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/goes/september-2017s-intense-solar-activity-viewed-from-space/) | ESA/NASA/SOHO/Joy Ng. Zero-based frame 17, September 10, 2017 16:12 UTC; resized to 800×800. The occulting disk blocks the Sun so its outer atmosphere can be seen. |
| `particles.jpg` | Same SOHO animation and explanation | ESA/NASA/SOHO/Joy Ng. Zero-based frame 19, September 10, 2017 18:12 UTC; resized to 800×800. Speckles are energetic particles hitting SOHO’s imager, not a photograph of individual particles flying in space. |

The particle-image timestamp is separate from the GOES13 16:25 UTC detection in
DONKI. The 19-minute game fact uses the interval from flare peak (16:06 UTC) to
that GOES detector event; it is not a SOHO-image interval or lunar arrival estimate.
The CME record start time (16:09 UTC) is separate from the 16:12 UTC image.
Artificial orbit rings, moving dust, sparks, colors of the interface, and repair
effects are game graphics. They are not measured NASA phenomena.
