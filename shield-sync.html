Real NASA story · shielding vs. game effects

Water and other hydrogen-rich materials can help shield against solar particles. This game imagines a water-wall system whose pump controls need calibration. The timing dial, pump locks, instant readiness, shield arcs, and scores are fictional mechanics, not a real shielding design or radiation model.

The backdrop is archived NASA/GSFC/SDO imagery of the September 10, 2017 X8.2 flare. Arcs show game progress; there is no force field or frequency setting that blocks solar radiation.
